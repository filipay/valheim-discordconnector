# Server-observed activities: design, implementation paused

**Current status: proposed revised scope; implementation PAUSED pending review.** Latest direction makes simulation-dependent stats optional across DiscordConnector installations, adds optional rack/oven completions and requests focused logic testing before separately authorized manual live validation. The simulation mod is **server-only; players install nothing**. The [authoritative plan](../plans/2026-10-26-server-only-discord-leaderboards.md) owns the six-task breakdown. Baseline: `e7dbbbc0fa322f28e52ad105ca61dcba0dfdf1b0`. Parent owns review/validation; no execution authority, app work, tests/builds/game execution or deployment is implied.

**Research history:** [feasibility and food](../../research/2026-10-26-activity-feasibility-and-food.md) resolves the creator representation bridge **statically**. Its companion alternatives are historical/excluded under the now-hard server-only boundary, not outstanding approval questions. Earlier four-activity adapter contracts and resource budgets below carry forward; the new plan changes combat semantics and internal report/storage contracts explicitly.

## Revised server-only scope — authoritative over older combat/food wording

**Hard boundary:** dedicated-server plugin only, compatible with ordinary crossplay clients. No client mod, companion, custom client messages, Jotunn dependency/service or new client RPC contract. Known vanilla routed methods use game `GetStableHashCode`. Exact food-eaten/popularity, cauldron/personal recipe crafting/upgrades, damage taken and parries remain excluded, not replaced by animation/gear proxies. **Optional rack/oven cooking completions are now in scope**; crops/other machine outputs remain future work.

**Proposed boards:** creature/boss participation credits, distinct deaths/types/biomes, direct HP damage, observed rolls, optional rack/oven completions and retained activity boards; experimental dodge avoidance remains separately gated. There are **14 report sections** (previous13 +cooking); optional output-prefab popularity uses the same completion records within spare cooking-section space, not another collector/promised board. All metrics default off; capture is independent of reports, with unavailable coverage as a reason rather than fabricated zero.

**Optional capability groups:** audited MistrCech GUID `MVP.Valheim_Serverside_Simulations`, source v1.24.0/pin `b2deeb7ebf1efdac2a09394da1984f08c7da5469`, is **not mandatory for DiscordConnector startup or players**. Legacy boards and independent rolls/sailing/inferred pickups/construction/runtime-grid-conditional exploration retain their own flags/guards without simulation/combat gating. Participant kills/direct damage require own flag +existing combat master +detected/enabled simulation +actual eligible server-owned victim. Cooking and experimental attack-side avoidance require their own flags +detected/enabled simulation +actual server-owned station/attack, **not combat master**; avoidance also needs its adapter gate. See [capability matrix](../plans/2026-10-26-server-only-discord-leaderboards.md#optional-simulation-capability). Reuse/extract the existing availability check, no hard mod reference/handshake/dependency framework. Missing/disabled mod resets dependent transient state and starts fresh epochs on re-enable; independent stats continue, stored historical reads/config remain unchanged, no backfill/startup crash/standalone empty-board spam. Concise coalesced coverage notes may accompany valid stored rows; presence does not promise completeness.

**Rack/oven completion contract:** validated sender +successful owner-local `RPC_AddItem` before/after snapshots must prove one unambiguous matching **free→input** slot. Persist world/station/slot generation, captured inserter, input, owner and collector epoch before accepting owner-side successful `UpdateCooking` **NotDone→Done** for that generation. Credit actual validated conversion/output-prefab units once; a known native one-item conversion may prove one, but never default unknown yields to1 or multiply by request/retrieval amount. No ingredient inventory-debit, eating or cauldron-crafting claim. Bounds: ≤32 slots/station and ≤4,096 tracked active slots/world within candidate limits. Unknown/preexisting slots and unfinished prior-restart epochs receive no credit; identical item names do not prove continuity. Removal/reuse, world/owner mismatch, capability loss/disable invalidate pending provenance. Direct NotDone→Burnt counts0; Done followed by burn remains one completed output, collected or not. Atomically persist slot completion +generation-unique actor/output credit; replay/restart cannot duplicate it. `SpawnItem`'s headless crafter0/empty and ItemLedger's unconditional owner-postfix `Fed` are **not** insertion/completion evidence.

**Participation qualification:** a unique stable socket account must cause an attributable, successfully applied **finite positive actual HP loss** on a currently server-owned untamed nonplayer creature. No proximity, request amount, final-blow-only rule or `s_attackers + name` credit. One qualified account receives one credit per confirmed world/victim death. Three contributors mean **one distinct death / three player credits**; creature-type/biome death totals use the death collection, not summed credits. Existing combat enablement/simulation-mod eligibility remains required and actual ownership is checked per observation.

Confirm completed eligible `OnDeath` at zero HP with successful original/enclosing finalizers, independently of whether the finisher was attributable. Previously qualified contributors can receive credit after an environmental/unattributed finish; attacker-less DoT creates no new actor attribution. Terminal zero-HP/deferred-death witnesses expire after **60 seconds**, without restarting an expired witness on a later callback. A fresh standalone successful zero-HP death callback may establish its own terminal witness if none was observed; absence of a witnessed final attacker is not a rejection reason. Removal alone is never death proof. Preserve the old lethal-hit correlation for historical last-hit data, but do not reuse its attributable-lethal prerequisite to gate participation.

**Ledger choices (agent proposals for review):** at most **1,024 victims**, **32 accounts/victim**, **8,192 contributor entries/world**, with each contributor expiring **10 minutes after its last qualified observed damage**. Reject new entries at capacity; do not evict another contributor, replay rejected hits or manufacture history. A later real qualifying hit can requalify an expired actor, with partial coverage disclosed. Retain immutable account/name/connection snapshot already accepted at the hit for delayed death credit **after disconnect**; never resolve it through a new connection/name/profile at death. New hits still require current unique connected attribution. Clear victim participation on ownership loss/change, revival, observed full heal (finite current max HP), invalid lifecycle/world switch or disable; full-heal resets credits, not already recorded damage. Rate-limit low-cardinality drop/expiry/coverage reasons; ledger/tombstone state stays within victim cap.

**Damage:** `min(preHP, max(0, preHP - postHP))` only for finite eligible same-owner observations with preHP>0, successful original/all enclosing invocations and attributable direct attacker. Capture zero-HP death snapshot before pooling if destruction prevents a safe post-read. Requests/HitData/overkill totals are not measured damage. Conservatively drop new same-victim nested damage observations when allocation is ambiguous; never count both outer and inner HP loss. Damage can count without a kill and can exceed starting HP across later healing; it is not damage taken or complete indirect damage. One coordinated `ApplyDamage` capture feeds legacy correlation and the new ledger/damage pipeline, not competing patches/double writes.

**Additive persistence:** `combat_participation_v1_deaths` and `combat_participation_v1_contributors` are atomically committed together, store-derived unique world/victim and world/victim/account keys; `combat_direct_damage_v1` stores uniquely keyed accepted direct deltas. No migration/backfill from one-last-hitter `combat_kills`; no joining/summing old and new kill credits. Old last-hit records remain separate historical/compatibility data, hidden from the new participation report (no duplicate competing legacy section). A restart loses unflushed/in-memory participation, so coverage starts with observation, not lifetime; committed duplicates cannot add credits. Legacy five boards/data/config and existing combat config keys remain intact.

**Dodge:** validate vanilla routed `SetTrigger("dodge")` against actual ingress connection/current player-character target, dedup message/local/broadcast observation once and label **observed rolls**. Avoidance requires server-executed attack/projectile collision rejection specifically due to dodge invulnerability, an eligible current defender, and one local action/projectile+defender dedup key. Replicated invulnerability can be stale. `RPC_HitWhileDodging` alone and `Aoe`/generic `RPC_Damage` paths do not establish that shared action evidence; no full avoidance coverage promise. If the later gate cannot prove a useful safe subset, leave avoidance unavailable/off with diagnostic, without disabling roll counts.

**Reporting/constraints:** retain shared LiteDB5/store-local UTC mapping and one serial database worker, bounded immutable main-thread capture, existing 32 sample units/4,096 cell visits per frame, 128-document batches/256 queued batches, 10s flush, calendar/claim/no-backlog semantics and existing `combat-report:v1:` keys. One literal no-mentions message ≤1,900 UTF-16 units/period, Dublin calendar/DST, top5 as a maximum. Reserve truthful qualifiers plus one row per enabled nonempty supported section before adding ranked rows; deterministic truncation, no pagination/extra messages. The new plan specifies current schemas, flags, APIs, tests and conditional avoidance gate; lower sections are retained detailed activity contracts, not authority to restore excluded features or old last-hit report sections.

**Approved local choices:** bind `[Server-Observed Activities]` in native `nwesterhausen.DiscordConnector.cfg` with four independent booleans `Sailing Enabled`, `Floor Pickups Enabled`, `Construction Enabled`, `Exploration Enabled`, all **false**. Enablement is not evidence of support: emit a rate-limited reason when prerequisites are absent.

Add proposed native **`Cooking Completions Enabled=false`** under the same activity section; capability-gated as above, not a mandatory dependency for the other four flags. Other new default-off combat/action flags are in the authoritative plan.

All collectors require an active dedicated-server world with nonzero world UID. Representation uncertainty is closed for this build; actual missing/ambiguous runtime mappings still reject candidates and can block useful coverage.

Sender validation proves a connection, not truthful client behavior.

### Approved pickup inference and provenance

Keep pickup confidence separate from source provenance: every remote candidate is `inferred-pickup`; provenance is `proven-fresh-source`, `inferred-source`, `known-player-drop/refund`, or `unknown/mixed`. A newly seen ZDO, creator-less item, nearby tree/corpse, owner request, or probable loot prefab **does not prove fresh gathering**. Only a server-observed fresh-producing action tied to the actual new drop ZDO/quantity, followed without any unobserved transfer, qualifies for a proven-fresh-source subset; its remote pickup classification is still inferred. Known player drops/refunds are never fresh; unknown or mixed provenance is never promoted. Display **“inferred floor pickups — source may be unknown”**, separately qualifying any proven-fresh-source subtotal; omit that subtotal when none are proven. Do not call inferred pickups exact transfers or “resources gathered.”

For a proven fresh source, persist a world/item lineage marker and remaining credited source quantity atomically with inferred pickup records. A redrop creates a new unknown/player-drop lineage, never new freshness; an attributable repick may count as an inferred floor pickup, never as another fresh gather. Merge participation taints the whole candidate: reject the affected pickup/fresh attribution rather than apportioning guessed quantities. On restart, only durable source evidence can remain fresh; loss of a cache entry is not evidence of freshness. Never reconstruct provenance from vicinity or reconcile hidden bags by guesswork. No primary release promise requires a fresh-source board when runtime provenance cannot actually be proven.

**Concrete correlation choice:** requests expire after **5 seconds**; cache at most **4,096 items**. A matching owner grant and a newer accepted reduction from that peer can stage `beforeStack - afterStack > 0`. A validated destroy from the same peer can stage the last valid remainder only if the request/grant is already known, item snapshot predates destruction, and no intervening owner/actor conflict occurred. Destroy-before-request/grant is suppressed, not retroactively credited. `RPC_DestroyZDO` alone provides no reason or acceptance proof. Snapshot before pool release; freeze each staged mutation's identity/revision and let later partial/deletion signals consume only its uncredited remainder. Replayed revisions and repeated destroy notifications never stage another event.

Hold staged reductions/deletions for a further **2-second exclusion window**, keeping the entire candidate lifecycle bounded to seven seconds. Observe local `AutoStackItems`, `TimedDestruction`, `RemoveOne`/consumption and refund/drop context; suppress any affected candidate. Track compatible observed donor deletion/destination stack increase within the window as merge evidence even when packets reverse order; competing requests, missing snapshots, mismatched item signatures and unvalidated sender context suppress credit. No quantity counts for a grant with unchanged stack/full inventory. Context absence is not proof, but a complete request/grant/mutation sequence without observed conflicts may mature as **inferred**, as approved. Unseen remote merges, consumption, late packets beyond this bounded window or deceptive client state can still cause misclassification; report that residual risk, not certainty. Do not wait for an exact client inventory-success witness or introduce a companion contract.

### New-piece baseline and delayed fields

At world readiness, baseline existing world ZDO identities, not just loaded scene `Piece` instances. Establish this before accepting new placements; if the registry cannot be baselined consistently, construction stays disabled with a diagnostic. Avoid scanning all world IDs every sample: maintain the one startup/world baseline and lifecycle hooks. A bounded incremental baseline may conservatively exclude arrivals until complete; report that startup blind window rather than counting uncertain IDs as new.

Resolve each candidate's prefab and require an actual `Piece` component before counting construction; creator presence alone is insufficient. The dashboard's `CountPieces` has no such component filter and is a standing-state census, not a placement event. Never use periodic full-world census/deltas to replace lifecycle tracking. On world switch clear identity and baseline caches before collecting again; do not copy the reference's retained first-name builder cache. Existing/reloaded/replaced objects are not new placements merely because a view instantiates or counts change.

Classify newly received ZDOs after successful ingress deserialization and locally created ZDOs after valid prefab/creator data becomes available. `Piece.Awake`/`ZNetScene.CreateObject` are only opportunities to resolve prefab metadata, not creation evidence. Keep new candidates for up to **30 seconds** for creator/cheated fields; re-evaluate before persistence. `SetCreator` can run before the placement's cheated flag, so do not insert from its immediate postfix. If a remote source can supply arbitrarily delayed contradictory flags, this cannot establish exact no-cost exclusion: the output remains an estimate and that limitation must be visible. Do not claim all cheated resources are detectable. Detectable no-build-cost world settings/local cheat context exclude estimates. Baseline on re-enable/world switch; durable world/ZDO dedup prevents re-observation after restart from counting again.

### Exploration grid and durable cells

Read **active runtime** `Minimap.m_textureSize`, `m_pixelSize`, `m_exploreRadius`; require positive finite values and checked work bounds. Concrete local limits are texture size **16,384** and circle radius **128 cells**; reject excess rather than clamping. No instance/settings on a headless server means **disabled with one diagnostic**, not constants from field initializers. Parent must establish the installed runtime exposes the grid; absence is a deployment prerequisite, not permission to invent it. Grid identity includes algorithm version, texture size, pixel size and radius using invariant exact representations. Persist the world's first grid definition. On mismatch pause exploration for that world instead of silently recounting the map in a new namespace.

Match G5: `centerX = Utils.RoundToInt(x / pixelSize + textureSize / 2)` and likewise `z`; `r = ceil(exploreRadius / pixelSize)`. Visit integer offsets satisfying `dx² + dy² <= r²`, clipping both axes to `[0,textureSize)`. Use checked/wide arithmetic and reject invalid input before integer conversion. Do not substitute floor/round-away-from-zero. Sample every **2 seconds**, credit only observed circles, and reset stale snapshots on gaps/ownership/world changes. A teleport paints the observed destination only, not its intervening route. This does not recreate the actual client fog state.

**Agent choice:** sparse **32×32 chunks**, not one LiteDB document/write per cell or one unbounded world-player blob. Each chunk has a 1,024-bit membership bitmap and packed `(localCellIndex, firstObservedUtcTicks)` entries, at most 1,024 entries. Preserve the earliest timestamp and never rewrite it on revisits. First observation means this collector's observation, even if the player explored the area years earlier. Expose coverage from installation/enablement, not lifetime exploration.

## Persistence, lifecycle and bounded work

Use existing `records.db` and initialization/disposal coordination. New store-local UTC-tick mapping mirrors `CombatStore`; no global mapper registration. Hash length-prefixed exact identifiers for keys and compare world/player values ordinally despite possible legacy case-insensitive collation.

|---|---|
| `activity_observations` | Typed pickup/piece/sailing records, world, account/name, observation UTC; prefab/quantity/provenance/source IDs or recipe-estimate map; sailing start/end UTC and monotonic seconds. IDs derived in the store: piece world/ZDO; pickup world/ZDO/accepted token; sailing collector-session GUID + sequence. Index world/time/type/player; range-query sailing by overlapping interval, not endpoint alone. No raw packet/position history. |

| `activity_exploration_chunks` | World/account/grid/chunk key, bitmap, bounded packed first timestamps, name metadata, min/max first UTC ticks for candidate-range queries. Transactionally merge against persisted membership; no read-overwrite race may lose bits/timestamps. |

Keep `combat_reports` as the **single claim collection**. No activity copy of the combat kill schema, no second report-claim namespace, legacy rewrite, automatic retention deletion or fallback DB. Store errors log exception type/reason counts without IDs, positions, paths or item payloads. Failed candidates are not reassigned. Queue overflow or ambiguous commit discards/invalidates affected volatile work, never promotes unknown lineage; durable uniqueness/membership controls any later re-observation. A crash may lose the most recent unflushed observations; it must not duplicate committed observations.

`Plugin.Update` first services activity sampling/results independently of report existence and the report minute gate. **Agent budgets:** at most 32 player/ship sample units and 4,096 exploration cell visits per frame, round-robin; if a unit misses its gap allowance reset its baseline, never catch up. Avoid per-frame world/prefab scans. Hook queues cap at 4,096 candidates; pending persistence at 256 bounded batches. Coalesce contiguous accepted sailing intervals for one world/ship/owner/user into spans of at most **10 seconds**; flush changed chunks and accepted observations at most every **10 seconds**, sooner on disable/world change/clean shutdown. Unprocessed circles retain their original timestamp; invalidated context discards unfinished work.

Database I/O is not hard-real-time. Use one serialized task tail owned by `ActivityStore`, not a separate worker framework/service: it handles immutable bounded batches, combined report aggregation and existing durable claims. No Unity API on that worker, no new dependency, generic event bus or full raw event ledger. Bound a batch to **128 observation/chunk documents** and paginate reads; do not rebuild full-history lists on the Unity thread. Commit each observation/source update or chunk merge atomically. Deliver only bounded aggregate/result snapshots to `Update`. Worker shutdown/drain precedes `Database.Dispose`; on disabled/world changes clear correlations and baselines immediately and flush only already accepted old-world snapshots under their captured world IDs. If drain fails, warn and lose pending work rather than touching a disposed DB. Cooperative budgets limit scheduled work, not guaranteed disk latency or absolute CPU time.

Current internal boundary is frozen by the new plan: nonblocking `ActivityStore.Enqueue(ActivityBatch)`, bounded worker `QueryAsync(world,startUtc,endUtc,options)`, and `TryClaimReportAsync(id,nowUtc)` wrapping the existing claim. ParticipantStore streams **new versioned** combat data; do not implement the older proposed `CombatStore.QuerySummary` last-hit input. Main-thread `ActivityRuntime.FlushThrough(endUtc)` flushes accepted snapshots and returns limitation strings. Same bounded worker/batch principles; full signatures and ownership are in the authoritative plan.

Tradeoff: short caches and batched accepted facts reduce writes and avoid retaining raw ZDO history, at the cost of explicit blind windows, rejected ambiguous actions and up to ten seconds of crash loss. Exploration's bounded timestamp chunks cost more than a plain bitmap but retain exact first-observation periods without per-cell writes; a bitmap alone cannot produce correct historical daily/weekly first-cell counts. Persistent accepted observations/chunks still grow with real activity; no retention policy is silently introduced.

## One combined daily/weekly report

Keep `CombatReports` as calendar/claim owner, extending its data input to bounded combat/activity aggregates. Preserve its existing `combat-report:v1:` claim identity exactly (world/timezone/period/bounds), including for activity-only reports. Changing enabled sections must never create another claim for that period. Preserve startup/world-change baseline, only latest completed period, no offline/backlog replay, Monday weeks and half-open UTC bounds. Keep Dublin DST 23/25-hour days and existing first-valid-gap/earlier-UTC-ambiguity rules.

Before querying a completed period, allow a **7-second grace** for pending inference to mature, then order a flush of accepted observations through its boundary; do not consume the pending period merely because grace has not elapsed. Query on the serialized database worker, then render and claim once before transport; new-period queries must not race a pre-boundary chunk flush. `CombatReports.Poll(nowUtc,world)` checks task completion each frame; existing `Tick` calendar checks remain minute-gated. Sampling continues while report aggregation runs. Calendar progress is reserved once per period while work is pending; do not queue historical jobs. Abandon unclaimed obsolete work if it falls behind the latest completed period. Failures do not trigger a retry/backlog; already claimed sends may be lost. Do not claim an empty report. Reports can use stored observations when collection flags are off, just as stored combat data can be reported independently of tracking.

For point observations and exploration use first/observation timestamp in `[start,end)`. Clip accepted sailing spans to the period; split their monotonic duration proportionally across the observed UTC span. Reset instead of recording spans across wall-clock discontinuities. Player grouping and tie breaks remain stable-account ordinal; name is latest observed display metadata. Never print stable account IDs. Counts are checked nonnegative integers; sailing retains fractional seconds internally and rounds only for display.

**Current output decision:** one message ≤**1,900 UTF-16 code units**, literal content/embed. Replace the fixed four × 380-character budgets with a total-budget allocator: reserve header plus a short heading/at least one safe row for each nonempty section, distribute the remainder, then add rows up to `Top Count` (default five). ## Secondary feasibility: precise limits, not ownership shorthand

Crop/other-machine ideas remain future scope; optional rack/oven completion is the exception now included in the proposed plan:

* **Crop harvest:** G6 `RPC_Pick(long,int)` includes the harvester request sender but executes only on the owning instance and rejects already-picked state. Forwarded client-owned requests can be visible through G7; that does not prove accepted harvest. A supported subset needs a validated sender, pre-unpicked state, completed accepted pick/`SetPicked` or owned destruction in that invocation, actual spawned yield and a reliable crop-prefab classification. `RPC_SetPicked` sender is the owner, not necessarily the harvester. Dedup world/pickable plus harvest generation (respawn cycle), not every broadcast. Missing request-success correlation, yield or classification remains unsupported; do not count every berry bush as a planted crop or substitute generic pickups for harvested crops.
* **Crop planting:** initial new `Plant` plus creator can follow construction's baseline/dedup contract, once crop vs tree/decorative prefab classification is verified. `Plant.Grow()` instantiates a replacement and destroys the original without copying creator: never infer a second planting or the harvester from that replacement. Growth replacements observed locally must be tagged/excluded; remote ambiguous replacements without evidence are not new plantings. Unit would be successfully observed planted crop pieces, not seeds consumed. Classification and client acceptance remain the bounded gaps.
* **Rack/oven food completed — optional proposed metric:** our accepted-input slot-generation tracker supplies missing inserter provenance and credits owner-side successful NotDone→Done output once. The contract/caps/restart/burn rules are above and in the plan. ItemLedger `Fed`, headless `SpawnItem` crafter fields, insertion requests and retrieval counts are not completion proof. Cauldron recipes/eating remain excluded; other machine outputs remain future scope.
* **Successful equipment upgrades:** local `InventoryGui.DoCrafting(Player)` supports success, break and quality reduction; replicated quality does not prove upgrade/debit/payer. Exact personal upgrades/crafting are excluded under the current hard server-only boundary. Prior companion alternatives are historical, not approval questions; no estimates disguised as spending.
