# Server-Only Discord Leaderboards Implementation Plan

Historical plan: its preserve-history policy is superseded only for the three reported-signal detail collections by the approved [November 3 retention design](../specs/2026-11-02-reporting-review-fixes-design.md). Other histories remain untouched.

**Status:** proposed plan only; implementation remains paused. Supersedes [the older activity plan](2026-10-25-server-activity-statistics.md). Latest direction makes simulation-dependent stats optional across DiscordConnector installations and asks for proportionate logic testing, then separately authorized manual live validation when ready. **MistrCech is installed on the server only; players need no mod.** Definitions/caps below are proposed engineering choices, not execution permission or runtime proof.
**Goal:** Deliver partial server-observed participation, damage, cooking-completion, roll and activity leaderboards, while normal DiscordConnector remains useful without the optional simulation mod.
**Architecture:** One coordinated combat capture and small pure cooking-slot tracker feed typed batches to the existing shared LiteDB worker/report scheduler. A simple reuse/extraction of existing simulation eligibility gates dependent collectors only; it is not a dependency framework. Experimental avoidance retains its adapter gate. No mandatory mod reference, client handshake or new service.
**Tech Stack:** Existing C#/BepInEx/Harmony/Unity, net481 plugin, LiteDB5.0.21, net8 console harnesses; no new dependency, service, Jotunn/hash catalog or client protocol.
**Spec:** [revised server-only scope](../specs/2026-10-25-server-activity-statistics-design.md#revised-server-only-scope--authoritative-over-older-combatfood-wording); [feasibility/exclusions](../../research/2026-10-26-activity-feasibility-and-food.md).

## Global constraints
- **Hard server-only/crossplay:** no client mod, companion, custom client messages or new client RPC. Vanilla traffic observation is allowed; embedded sender IDs still require actual connection validation. Use game `GetStableHashCode` for known method names.
- Preserve five legacy boards, data/config, public interfaces and existing combat config keys. Retain `CombatConfig.Enabled=false` and simulation-mod eligibility for creature combat, checking actual server ownership/untamed/nonplayer status per observation; new metrics also default off. Existing legacy last-hit data is retained, never migrated/backfilled into participation.
- Capture must work with daily/weekly reports disabled. Flags control metric collection and report inclusion; collection/inclusion snapshots are explicit, no hidden last-hit fallback. Turning a flag off does not delete data; re-enabling never reconstructs unobserved history. Restart after report-setting changes as before.
- Same `records.db`, additive versioned collections, store-local UTC ticks (no global mapper change), exact ordinal stable socket-account/world IDs, immutable captured names/time/identity. No automatic retention deletion or raw packet/position history.
- Existing work limits: **32 player/ship sample units +4,096 cell visits/frame**, hook/candidate cap **4,096**, flush **10s**, batches **≤128 physical documents**, **≤256 queued batches**; one serial database worker and bounded aggregates, no expensive main-thread history queries. Participation caps below are separate bounded state, not per-hit history.
- Existing activity definitions: sailing **1s /0.5m/s /2.5s gap**, spans≤10s; pickup **5s request +2s exclusion hold**; construction delayed fields≤30s; exploration **2s**, runtime-only grid, **32×32 chunks**, texture≤16,384/radius≤128cells. No weakened freshness or invented minimap defaults.
- Existing calendar: **Europe/Dublin**, Monday weeks, DST-safe `[startUtc,endUtc)`, same `combat-report:v1:` claim IDs, startup/world baseline/no backlog, durable claim before send, at-most-once **attempt**, not delivery; no automatic send retry.
- One **≤1,900 UTF-16 code-unit** literal `SendRenderedMessage(LeaderboardsAll,...)` per day/week, content/embed mentions disabled. Top **5** (existing range1–16) is a maximum, not five guaranteed rows/board. No pagination/extra messages/destinations.
- Exclude exact food-eaten/popularity, cauldron/personal recipe crafting/upgrades, damage taken and parries; no animation/gear proxy or companion question. Optional rack/oven **completions** are in scope below, not eating/crafting. Crops, other machine outputs and extra censuses remain future scope.
- No app work/tests/builds/game execution/git writes/deployment in this documentation turn. Future executable steps require explicit resumption; build artifacts are validation only, not authorized delivery/production installation.

## Metric contracts and bounded lifecycle

| Board | Qualification / units | Exclusions and reporting identity |
|---|---|---|
| Participation: creatures (nonboss) / bosses | One credit per unique stable account with a qualified actual positive direct HP-loss observation before a confirmed eligible victim death. | No final-blow-only requirement, proximity, request amount, name attacker marks or unobserved assists. Death timestamp places credits in report period. |
| Distinct deaths / creature types / death-location biomes | One successful zero-HP death callback per world/victim, untamed nonplayer, actually server-owned. | **3 contributors →1 death/3 credits**. Group these totals from death documents, never from summed contributor credits. No removal-only confirmation. |
| Direct observed damage | Finite `min(preHP,max(0,preHP-postHP))`, preHP>0, successful same-owner applied invocation, direct currently identifiable attacker. | Not HitData/requested damage or overkill; no reassigned attacker-less DoT. Damage counts without a kill, at its own observed UTC; healing can permit later additional real loss. |
| Observed dodge rolls | Validated vanilla routed `SetTrigger("dodge")` targeting the sender's **current character ZDO**, dedup one transport message across local/broadcast observations. | Observed roll triggers, not proof of avoided damage/invulnerability or cheat-proof physical action. Other targets/forged sender/malformed traffic0. |
| Experimental observed dodge avoidances | Only validated server-executed attack/projectile collision rejection **because of dodge invulnerability**, unique local action/projectile +defender. | Gate in Task5; partial coverage/replicated-state staleness. No counting `RPC_HitWhileDodging` alone, generic `RPC_Damage` or `Aoe` notification guesses. Roll count is independent. |
| Sailing, inferred pickups, Piece placements/estimated materials | Existing detailed observation contracts retained; identity/provenance/exclusions unchanged. | Inferred pickups remain explicitly uncertain; materials are standard recipe estimates, never actual spending. |
| Observed exploration | Runtime-grid-conditional per-world/account first-cell observations, restart dedup. | Not private client map history; missing/mismatched grid reports limitation, not zero/default values. |
| Rack/oven cooking completions | Credit the validated **accepted inserter** for actual supported food-output units at owner-side `NotDone→Done`, once per slot generation. | Not insertion attempts, retrieval, eating, cauldron recipes or ingredients debited. Unknown/preexisting/restart provenance and direct-to-burnt transitions get no credit. |

**Participation ledger proposals:** max **1,024 victim slots**, **32 accounts/victim**, **8,192 contributor entries/world**. Each contributor expires **10 minutes after its last qualified damage**; rejected/new entries do not evict others or get replayed later. Expired actors can requalify only by a new observed valid hit. Keep only account/name/connection snapshot, last-qualified time and minimal encounter state, not all hits. Expiry/cap/restart loss means partial coverage, never full lifetime.
**Disconnect decision:** once a hit qualifies with a current unique actor, retain its immutable account/name snapshot for later death credit after disconnect; do **not** resolve/rebind via a reconnect/name/profile at death. Every new hit requires current actor validation. Same account's repeated hits still yield one credit/death.
**Reset decision:** invalidate victim participation/pending confirmation on owner loss/change, revival, observed full heal to finite current maxHP, invalid lifecycle/world switch or metric disable. Full heal clears participation, not already measured damage. On ZDO destruction clear only after a successful death snapshot has been staged; disappearance alone adds nothing.
**Death proof:** independent successful `OnDeath` zero-HP/server-owned snapshot +original/enclosing-finalizer success. A successful terminal zero-HP observation can be followed by deferred death ≤**60s**; expired terminal witnesses cannot be refreshed by a late callback. A fresh standalone eligible death callback can establish a terminal witness when no earlier one was observed. Environmental/unattributed finish still credits retained qualified contributors; it adds no new contributor. Drop invalid/expired confirmation instead of guessing.
**Nested damage policy:** stage until every enclosing invocation succeeds. Conservatively discard new **same-victim nested** HP deltas when allocation is ambiguous, rather than credit both outer and inner; valid independent earlier contributions remain. Failed/skipped originals invalidate affected staged work, not unrelated finalized observations. A captured valid zero-HP death snapshot may replace unavailable post-damage state after pooling.
**Bounded failure behavior:** reject new victim/account entries at cap and count low-cardinality drop/expiry reasons; no replacement credit. Pending terminal/invalid-lifecycle tombstones consume the victim cap and expire only into a closed/no-credit state until that lifecycle is removed/reset, never a fresh late-death credit. Hook queue overflow suppresses affected staged observations. Maintain expiry incrementally within main-thread work budget; acceptance checks TTL even if sweeping lags.

**Cooking proof and units:** validate sender/current actor at accepted `RPC_AddItem(long,string,bool)` and snapshot bounded slots before/after the successful original/enclosing invocation. Require one unambiguous matching **free→input** slot on the same server-owned station; a request/postfix alone proves nothing. Mint world/station/slot generation with captured actor, input, owner and collector epoch, persist it in queue order, then accept successful owner-side `UpdateCooking` **NotDone→Done** only for that generation and validated conversion/output prefab/units. A native one-item conversion may prove one unit; do not use1 as a generic default, multiply by request/retrieval amount or invent modded/bonus yields. Unknown output quantity/conversion is skipped with coverage reason. Optional output-prefab popularity can reuse these records later/within spare cooking-section space; no extra collector or promised extra board.
**Cooking lifecycle:** ≤**32 slots/station**, ≤**4,096 active tracked slots/world** within existing candidate limits; reject excess, do not guess. Clear/free/removal/reuse, owner/world mismatch, capability loss or disable invalidate pending provenance; completed credits remain immutable. A direct NotDone→Burnt transition counts0; a witnessed Done followed by burn still counts its completion **once**, whether collected or not. Capture inserter identity at acceptance; no name/reconnect rebind at completion. **Restart choice: invalidate all unfinished prior-epoch provenance**, even if a slot looks identical; retain durable generation/credit dedup but never infer continuity. Preexisting slots are unknown until a new accepted free→input transition. Atomic slot-state +completion/credit persistence prevents duplicate completion across replay/restart; no ingredient inventory-debit claim.
