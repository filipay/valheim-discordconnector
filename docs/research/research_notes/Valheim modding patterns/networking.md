## Derived recommendations and limits

Prefer native typed arguments, then decoded routed fields, then raw parsing only where necessary. Decode only the schema at that layer; own bytes before deferral. Bound sizes before allocations, preserve cursors and normal routing, authenticate provenance separately from permissions and outcome, and fence stale sessions. A global/local handler cannot cover every forwarded request. Custom Jötunn/ServerSync protocols require matching endpoint implementations despite optional presence checks.

No raw wire schema or exact 1.0.21/PlayFab binary was independently inspected in this lane. No cited mod supplies a complete current-character/account authentication or universal exactly-once contract. NPS takeover is a compatibility consideration, not a proven cause of the live chat failure. No licensed implementation was transplanted.
